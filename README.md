# Ocular Motility Lab

**An interactive simulator for learning eye movements, strabismus, cranial nerve palsies, pupils and the red reflex.**

🔗 **Live demo:** https://ahmed-bashar12.github.io/Ophthalmology-/

Concept and design by **Ahmed Bashar** · Version 1.0 · First published 25 September 2026

---

## Overview

Ocular Motility Lab lets you see how the extraocular muscles work, alone and together, and how each disorder changes eye movement, eyelid position and pupil behaviour. Both eyes are shown from the examiner's view, with live measurements. It needs no installation and runs in any modern browser, on desktop or mobile.

## Features

### Eye movements
- **H-test and nine positions of gaze**: automatic sweeps with guide arrows and yoke-muscle labels, or Manual mode (click or drag the fixation light)
- **Single-muscle mode**: primary, secondary and tertiary actions, with the 23° and 51° muscle planes shown from above
- **Nine diagnostic gaze photos**: a clinical-style 3×3 montage for every condition
- **Examination tools**: cover, uncover and alternate cover tests; choice of fixing eye (primary vs secondary deviation); distance and near targets; Bielschowsky head tilt; room light
- **Live readouts**: deviation in degrees and prism dioptres, Hirschberg estimate, torsion, the patient's diplopia view, and versions grading (−4 to +4)

### Conditions
- Comitant strabismus: infantile esotropia, accommodative esotropia, intermittent exotropia, convergence insufficiency
- Isolated weakness of each extraocular muscle
- CN III (complete, pupil-sparing, divisional), CN IV and CN VI palsies; cavernous sinus / orbital apex syndrome
- INO, one-and-a-half syndrome, horizontal gaze palsy, dorsal midbrain (Parinaud) syndrome
- Duane types 1–3, Brown syndrome, thyroid eye disease, orbital floor fracture
- Horner syndrome, myasthenia gravis, aponeurotic and congenital ptosis

### Pupils and red reflex
- Penlight direct and consensual reflexes, swinging flashlight test (RAPD), near reflex and light–near dissociation
- Anisocoria work-up: Horner syndrome, Adie tonic pupil, CN III palsy, pharmacologic mydriasis and miosis, Argyll Robertson pupils
- Diagnostic drops: pilocarpine 0.1% and 1%, apraclonidine, cocaine, hydroxyamphetamine, and mydriatics
- Live pupillogram and reaction table
- Brückner red-reflex test: leukocoria (retinoblastoma), congenital cataract, strabismus, anisometropia, vitreous haemorrhage

## Intended use

Designed for medical students, ophthalmology residents, orthoptists and educators, for teaching, revision and exam preparation.

> ⚠️ **Educational use only.** This is a simplified teaching model. It is not a medical device and must not be used for diagnosis, treatment or any clinical decision. Always follow current clinical guidance.
